# Sample Bank Lending Guidelines

Demo lending guidelines for term, size, and coverage.

## §2 Facility size and term

Straight-through term is 7 years or less. A term above 10 years is a hard stop.

The proposed loan should be at or below 2.5x EBITDA for straight-through approval. Above 4.0x EBITDA is a hard stop.

Pro forma Debt/Equity should be at or below 3.5x for straight-through approval. Above 5.0x is a hard stop. Pro forma debt includes the proposed loan.

Eligible purposes include equipment, working capital, refinance, and owner-occupied premises. The purpose is recorded on the application and repeated in the credit memo.

## §3 Interest coverage

Interest coverage is EBITDA divided by interest expense. Straight-through approval expects coverage of at least 2.0x. Coverage below 1.25x is a hard stop. If interest expense is zero, coverage is not a constraint.
