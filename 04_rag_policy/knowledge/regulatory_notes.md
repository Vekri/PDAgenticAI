# Regulatory notes for the decision desk

These notes keep the demo inside ordinary consumer and commercial fairness practice. They are not a legal opinion.

## §1 Fair lending, reasons, and the audit trail

The model does not take race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, or age. Industry is a business segment, not a personal characteristic.

Each automated outcome needs specific reasons a credit officer can read. Approved files cite the straight-through tests. Reviewed and declined files cite the tests that failed, with the policy section.

The desk keeps an audit trail on this computer: inputs, ratios, PD, policy checks, retrieved passages, the recommendation, and the explanation. A person remains accountable for the credit decision. Review and exception cases are not straight-through.

Adverse-action language, when a decline is communicated to an applicant, must stay specific to the failed credit factors and must be reviewed by the bank's own compliance process.
